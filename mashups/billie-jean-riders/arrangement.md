Jake's arrangement (2026-11-02), being built in Logic Pro ("mashup take 2", ~/Music/Logic) from the three
tracks' recordings, at 117 bpm in E minor. Logic works only from copies in ~/Music/Logic/mashup-sources/,
never from tracks/ (Logic writes into any wav it opens).
