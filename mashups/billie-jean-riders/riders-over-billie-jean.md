# Riders on the Storm over Billie Jean's bass loops

Measured 2026-11-12 from the stems in `audio/stems/` (117 BPM, everything starting at bar 1).
Billie Jean is in E minor here: transposed −2 and detuned −40 cents in the Ableton set. Bar numbers
are Ableton bars of the Riders stems, give or take one bar.

The two Billie Jean loops:

- **E loop:** the bass plays E B D E D B A B, and the chord stabs use E F# G A B C# D (E Dorian, which is
  E minor with C#).
- **A loop:** the same riff on A (A E G A G E D E), under an A minor chord (A C E G), so this loop has
  **C**, not C#.

## What that means for your arrangement

- **Over the E loop:** almost any stretch of the Riders groove works. Bars **70–119** are the longest
  clean run, about 50 bars with nothing to dodge.
- **Over the A loop:** Riders' two-bar turnarounds fit: bars **30–31, 42–43, 54–55, 68–69 and 140–141**,
  each a D bar then a C bar. The loops change every two bars, and so does each turnaround, so one
  turnaround should cover one A loop.
- **Avoid** Riders' A-major bars (26, 38, 50, 64, 136) over either loop, or keep only Riders' drums there.
- **bj's pre-chorus** (bars 35–41 and 75–81 of bj, alternating C and E in the bass) suits Riders' C bars
  too.
- If you layer Riders' **bass** too, it doubles bj's and blurs the riff. The other stems (vocals, piano,
  other) layer more cleanly.
